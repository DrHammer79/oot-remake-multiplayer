Before it Navi bundled 83cb91eb6118 (protocol 11): in git history (this folder and setup/deps/hookshot_coop.dll).

What Navi takes: the co-op module (hookshot_coop.dll, built from hookshot/ with Visual Studio), the body pack builder
(tools/puppet_pack), its engine patches (hookshot/patches 0001-0021, fitted into Navi-main: Navi's own text input, menu
drawing, tab strip, pad and menu-state code kept where both did the same), coop_test (its CMake adds it whenever the
module is built), netsim and autotest (local testing), docs, and the kit's coop-options.ini and OOTRR.txt (defaults and
the pinned randomizer release; Navi does not run the kit's scripts).

Navi's changes to this copy:
- coop_hookshot.cpp: NAVI_COOP_HOST=1 hosts at the game's start (NAVI_COOP_NAME, NAVI_COOP_PASSWORD, NAVI_COOP_PORT,
  NAVI_COOP_PVP; 4 players) for Navi's Online tab, whose WebRTC link carries each friend's UDP (navi/tunnel.py UdpTunnel).
- coop_hookshot.cpp, coop_host_menu.{h,cpp}: NAVI_COOP_JOIN=1 and Menu::NaviConnected: the Multiplayer tab says Connected
  for a game Navi connected at its start (LICHII_COOP_SERVER), and its Leave ends it.
- coop/host/src/save_transfer.cpp (folder_choice), its header's comment, lichii/hook/coop_save.cpp (2026-11-15): a guest
  who joins with a save loaded plays the host's save too. It comes in at once and is switched to on the title's menu with
  no file loaded (pending_step, unchanged), never under a game being played; a chat line tells the guest to go back to
  the title. Upstream kept such a guest on its own save for the whole session (a friend who joined from the pause menu
  played his own early save).
- New Game+ (2026-11-15): lichii/hook/coop_ngplus.{h,cpp} (new; listed in core.cmake): Ganon's death (the game's event
  request) unlocks the next level and keeps the most hearts; the level is chosen on the Multiplayer tab's Settings page
  (hookshot/src/coop_pause_menu.{h,cpp}, coop_pause.cpp: a New Game+ row, Request kNgPlus) and kept in coop-ngplus.ini.
  With a level on, the kept hearts are put in MaxLife and our Link's Life component (coop_objects.{h,cpp} RaiseLinkMax),
  called from coop_hook.cpp in a session and alone. coop/protocol difficulty.hpp ngplus_difficulty (life +50%, damage
  +25% a level, within every end) is applied by the server (server/session.{hpp,cpp} set_ngplus, enet_server.hpp's
  session(), host_service.{hpp,cpp} set_ngplus, called each round by hookshot/src/coop_host_menu.cpp) and alone by
  lichii/hook/coop_difficulty_game.cpp Now(). That file's Packed/Unpacked also keep the bosses' two values now (they
  held three of five, so boss life and damage never reached the game and stayed 100%).

- Solo or multiplayer saves (2026-11-15): lichii/hook/coop_save.{h,cpp} ChooseSaveSet / MultiplayerSaves and the first
  title's NoteTitle: the multiplayer saves are <account>-mp beside the solo ones (made as a copy of them the first time;
  a guest's copy of the host's save is then <account>-mp-coop), switched with the module's remount on the title's menu
  with no file loaded and no session, kept in coop-saves.ini. hookshot/src/coop_pause_menu.{h,cpp}, coop_pause.cpp: the
  Settings page's Saves row (Request kSaveSet).

Navi's change beside it, in Navi-main (not this copy): host/coop_voice.c opens the microphone on a thread of its own
(turning voice on froze a game when the open blocked the present thread).
