# hs-dbno-adult (row 113 parts 1-4, the ADULT case): adults have no crawl animations (Tunnel_* are in the child's banks

# A hosts (joins first), sets the bleed-out to 10 s and stays up in Hyrule Field (adult, by Kakariko's steps); B is
# downed by the game's own hits (hurt 4 x3), lies, tries to move (the stick must not move it), bleeds out, spectates A,
# then respawns beside A once no enemy is near and stands up. See hs-dbno-adult.B.txt for B's checks.

# Shots by eye: B-lying / B-stick (B flat on the ground, not a T or bind pose; A-* shots: B's puppet flat too),
# B-after-respawn (B standing beside A).
