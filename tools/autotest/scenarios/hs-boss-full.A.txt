# hs-boss-full (boss-autotest; player reports #30 #31 #55 #88 #150): a whole Gohma fight with the game's own
# paths only. Test-only steps (tools/autotest/hookshot.py + lichii/hook/coop_testinput.h): `until` waits for her
# opening (her animation, from the module's `watch`), `aim` turns the camera with the right stick onto her eye (an
# offset in her own frame through the game's camera matrix); then real ZL/ZR/Y presses lock on, fire the slingshot
# and swing the sword, so the stun, chance time, the eye hits and her death are her own AI's and the game's own Life
# step's in each game. `difficulty 300 100 100` is the host menu's own call (bosses 300%). The test Links get 20
# hearts (setflag PlayerStatus.MaxLife/Life 80), refilled every 3 rounds.
# A (host, walks in first and under her: her drop) owns her; B (guest) walks in ~25 s later (its own intro, a
# copy). Rounds 1-3: only B fires (the stun and the hand-over are the guest's); A swings when she is down.
# SAVE: both lanes' baselines at quest Get_StoneOfForest = GoToBossRoom (save_flags.py set ...
#   enum:0x7d776b61=0xb5df43cb); a later step makes her prefab delete her.
# Run: tools\autotest\run.ps1 -Runtime hookshot -Lane 1 -Scenario hs-boss-full -Timeout 1500
#      -Server <coop-kit\auto-B\server\coop_server.exe>   (the 0.11 server)

#  0. VOID unless she drops after a-trigger: her anim leaves Pre_Battle_Ceiling_* "(ours)" and A logs "ready to own
#     the boss".
#  1. A "coop: test difficulty custom 300 100 100: set"; the 5 s line "difficulty: bosses: ... (boss life 300%)";
#     her life lines read "of 63" (21 x 3).
#  2. "coop: test until ... seen" and "aim on Enemy_DungeonBoss_Gohma" lines in both drivers.
#  3. STUN by B: B "our own hit: the copy runs its own reaction", B "Enemy_DungeonBoss_Gohma: now ours" (hand-over),
#     her Down_Start in BOTH logs (shots b-rN-seed / a-rN-seed).
#  4. EYE HITS by both: her life falls in the owner's game for A's and B's swings ("replays a hit" in the owner,
#     "share damage" in the hitter); about 3x the stuns of a 100% fight.
#  5. ONE DEATH: "boss beaten" once per game; each copy "its owner's died: the copy's own Life step lets it die now";
#     her death cutscene in both (shots a-dead / b-dead); never "lost kill" / "killed here, owner still shows life".
